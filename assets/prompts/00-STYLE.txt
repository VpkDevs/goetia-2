DEMONICON — SHARED STYLE BLOCKS
================================
Append the relevant block to every prompt. Keep it byte-identical across a batch
so the set looks like one family. Change ONE thing at a time.

dark fantasy occult game icon, single centered object, isolated on pure black
background, flat shading with strong rim light, bold readable silhouette,
obsidian and black marble materials, ACCENT_COLOR emissive glow, bone-white
highlights, high contrast, subtle bloom, centered square composition, crisp
edges, no text, no letters, no watermark, no border, no frame

dark fantasy occult portrait, infernal nobility, regal and composed not

--- [KEYART] — marketing illustration ---
dark fantasy occult ARPG key art, isometric diorama, near-black cathedral-archive
interior, black marble and obsidian architecture, ritual machinery, ACCENT_COLOR
emissive light as the only light source, heavy bloom, hundreds of small glowing
projectiles, dramatic scale, cinematic, stylized flat-shaded 3D look, no text,
no watermark, no logo

--- HARD RULES ---
* NOT horror. This is infernal *nobility* and *cosmic bureaucracy*. Elegant,
  ceremonial, administrative-occult. No gore, no jump-scare imagery.
* Light is information. If something glows, it means something.
* Everything must read as a silhouette at 48 px. Squint test every icon.
* Never bake UI frames/borders into an icon — the engine draws those.
